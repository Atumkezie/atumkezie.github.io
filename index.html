GraphPad Prism stores a surprising number of things on your computer: your project data, your preferences, the place where exported charts land, and a cache of files it builds while you work. 🧹 Keeping the Cache Under Control

Caches are helpful. They make repeat exports faster because the software does not have to rebuild everything from scratch. The trouble starts when a cache grows for months without anyone noticing, quietly filling space on a laptop that was already getting full.

This helper surfaces the export cache so you can see how much it holds. When you decide it is time to clear it, the utility keeps the action deliberate rather than automatic, so nothing disappears before you are ready. Clearing a cache never touches your saved projects or your finished exports.
